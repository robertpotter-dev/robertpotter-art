— creative studio & practice

Robert Potter Art is a creative studio dedicated to archival portraiture and visual design. Most photographs in the digital age are transient. These are made to remain: archival pigment on cotton fiber, framed by hand, and composed for the architecture, light, and scale of the spaces they inhabit.

Because of the unhurried focus this medium requires, commissions are intentionally limited. A sitting is a deliberate process, undertaken when the occasion asks for one. Letters are received with the weight they carry.
